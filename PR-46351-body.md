### Summary

`TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/Spanning.lean` gains three declarations in `Matrix.GeneralLinearGroup`.

- `exists_eq_smul_map_of_span_eq_top`: let `f : O →+* L` be injective, with `[IsDomain O]` and `[IsBezout O]`, and let the `T i` span `M_n(O)`. If `a : GL n L` has `a⁻¹ * (T i).map f * a ∈ Set.range (Matrix.map · f)` for every `i`, then `a = α • k.map f` for some `α : Lˣ` and `k : GL n O`. This is §2.2 (c). A coefficient ring of the roadmap is a discrete valuation ring, hence a Bézout domain.
- `inv_apply_mul_apply_mem_range_of_span_eq_top`: under the spanning and conjugation hypotheses, every product `a⁻¹ i j * a k l` lies in the image of `f`. It follows from conjugating the matrix units, and needs no hypothesis on `O` or `f`.
- `exists_eq_smul_map_of_inv_apply_mul_apply_mem_range`: over a Bézout domain, a matrix with this property is a unit of `L` times the image of an element of `GL_n(O)`. Write `a⁻¹ i j * a k l = f (p i j k l)` and fix `i₀, j₀` with `p i₀ j₀ j₀ i₀ ≠ 0`. The ideal generated by the `p i₀ j₀ k l` has a generator `d = ∑ r (k, l) * p i₀ j₀ k l`, with `p i₀ j₀ k l = c k l * d`. Then `α = ∑ r (k, l) * a k l` is the scalar: `α • a⁻¹` is the image of an integral matrix `D`, and cancelling `d` in `O` gives `p i j k l = D i j * c k l`, so `a = α • c`. The integral matrices `c` and `D` are inverse to each other. No cancellation takes place in `L`.

`TauCeti/Data/Matrix/Basis.lean` gains `Matrix.mul_single_one_mul_apply`, the entry formula `(X * single j r 1 * Y) u v = X u j * Y r v`, next to the other matrix-unit product identities there. It is a `@[simp]` lemma.

Two departures from the roadmap's wording. The hypothesis is that each conjugate lies in the image of `M_n(O)`, in place of a second family `T′`; the two are equivalent. And `O` is any Bézout domain, in place of a coefficient ring. The proof does not follow [ShortProof]'s `New.Selmer.exists_eq_smul_of_conj_eq`, which was not consulted: it replaces the entry of maximal absolute value by a generator of a finitely generated ideal.
